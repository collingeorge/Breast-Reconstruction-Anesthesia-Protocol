# 01 — Preoperative Assessment & Checklist

> **Educational work by a pre-medical student — not medical advice.** Not a clinical protocol, prescription, or patient handout, and not for patient care. See [DISCLAIMER](DISCLAIMER.md).

### Post-mastectomy breast implant revision — educational dosing reference, November 2026

**How to read the numbers.** This is an educational template showing the parts of a perioperative assessment. Every dose, threshold or timing carries a tag such as [S6](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s6). The tag links to the source in [DOSING_SOURCES.md](DOSING_SOURCES.md). The values show what that guideline, label or score paper states. They are illustrative only. A treating clinician individualizes them under institutional policy. This is not an order set, and it has not been validated for this procedure.

*Values changed from v2.0, and v2.0 values removed for lack of an acceptable source, are listed in [RELEASE_NOTES.md](RELEASE_NOTES.md).*

### 5.2 PONV risk — Apfel simplified score
Source: [S9](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s9). One point each:
- [ ] Female sex
- [ ] Non-smoker
- [ ] History of PONV or motion sickness

| Risk factors | Estimated PONV risk [S9](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s9) | Prophylaxis intensity (adults) |
|---:|---:|---|
| 0 | ~10% | Consider risk reduction; prophylaxis individualized [S7](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s7) |
| 1 | ~21% | Two interventions [S7](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s7), [S8](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s8) |
| 2 | ~39% | Two interventions [S7](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s7), [S8](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s8) |
| 3 | ~61% | Three to four interventions [S7](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s7) |
| 4 | ~79% | Three to four interventions [S7](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s7) |

The Apfel score estimates risk. It does not choose the anesthetic. The fifth consensus also lists other risk factors, such as younger age, volatile anesthetic, nitrous oxide and longer anesthesia [S8](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s8).

## 6. Regional anesthesia considerations (PECS I/II or serratus plane block)

### 6.1 Contraindications and cautions
- [ ] Infection at the block site
- [ ] Patient refusal
- [ ] Allergy to amide local anesthetics
- [ ] Antithrombotic therapy or coagulopathy. This is a *relative* concern for superficial, compressible plane blocks. Apply the ASRA guideline by agent, dose, timing and block depth [S36](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s36).
- [ ] Planned IV lidocaine infusion. IV lidocaine is **not** given concurrently with a block or infiltration. The consensus separates the two by about 4 h [S1](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s1).

### 6.2 Planned regional technique
- [ ] PECS I + II
- [ ] Serratus anterior plane block
- [ ] No block (patient preference or contraindication)

The 2026 ASA guideline addresses fascial plane blocks for mastectomy, not implant revision specifically [S37](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s37). Evidence from one breast-surgery population should not be read as proof of benefit in every revision.

## 8. Perioperative medication plan (illustrative label and guideline values)

### 8.1 Oral premedication / multimodal analgesia
- [ ] **Acetaminophen** 1000 mg (adults ≥50 kg). Maximum 4000 mg/day from all routes and products [S24](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s24). Contraindicated in severe hepatic impairment [S24](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s24).
- [ ] **Celecoxib**, if no renal, GI or cardiovascular contraindication. Label acute-pain regimen: 400 mg initially, then 200 mg if needed on day 1 [S25](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s25).
- [ ] **Gabapentinoid — not routine.** A 2020 meta-analysis found no clinically meaningful analgesic benefit and more dizziness and visual disturbance (Verret 2020; see [06](06_References_Evidence_Breast_Augmentation.md)). Perioperative analgesic use is off-label, and no acceptable dose source is cited, so **no dose is given here**.

### 8.2 Antibiotic prophylaxis
Source: [S6](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s6).

- [ ] **Cefazolin** 2 g IV, or 3 g if weight ≥120 kg, within 60 min before incision; redose every 4 h in prolonged surgery [S6](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s6)
- [ ] Reported penicillin allergy: follow the institutional allergy pathway. Cefazolin is usually considered unless there is a severe β-lactam reaction history.
- [ ] Severe β-lactam allergy: **clindamycin** 900 mg IV (redose every 6 h) [S6](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s6), or **vancomycin** 15 mg/kg IV started within 120 min before incision [S6](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s6)

## 9. Day-of-surgery checklist
- [ ] Identity confirmed with two identifiers
- [ ] Surgical and anesthesia consent documented. FDA-required breast implant Patient Decision Checklist reviewed (labeling approved in 2021 [S39](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s39)).
- [ ] Site marked and laterality confirmed
- [ ] Antibiotic timing planned: cefazolin or clindamycin within 60 min; vancomycin within 120 min before incision [S6](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s6)
- [ ] Premedications given as ordered
- [ ] Regional block performed if planned; local-anesthetic dose recorded for the cumulative total [S4](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s4), [S5](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s5)
- [ ] Implants and sizers available
- [ ] Airway equipment and difficult-airway cart confirmed
