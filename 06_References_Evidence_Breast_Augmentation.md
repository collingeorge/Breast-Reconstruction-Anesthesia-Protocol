# Evidence and citation audit

> **Educational work by a pre-medical student — not medical advice.** Not a clinical protocol, prescription, or patient handout, and not for patient care. See [DISCLAIMER](DISCLAIMER.md).
The previous bibliography contained abbreviated and insufficiently specified references and unsupported blanket conclusions. It is superseded by the following limited source set for the safety corrections in this revision. It is not a comprehensive breast reconstruction review.

## Sources and limits

- [Foo et al., 2021: IV lidocaine consensus](https://associationofanaesthetists-publications.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/anae.15270).
- [Beloeil et al., 2021: POFA randomized trial](https://pubs.asahq.org/anesthesiology/article-pdf/134/4/541/512443/20210400.0-00011.pdf).
- [Verret et al., 2020: perioperative gabapentinoid meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32667154/).

These sources do not validate an implant-revision-specific regimen. Source-specific populations, comparators, follow-up, and adverse effects must be checked before generalization.

## Evidence statements removed

Universal superiority of PECS blocks, universal safety or superiority of opioid-free anesthesia, fixed percentage improvements, informal A/B certainty grades, and claims of consensus for specific irrigation or capsulectomy strategies are not retained. They require source-specific assessment and cannot be established by a general guideline label.

For a separate narrative review with a broader bibliography, see the [Breast Reconstruction Analgesia Review](https://github.com/collingeorge/Breast-Reconstruction-Analgesia-Review). That project is not an institutional protocol.
