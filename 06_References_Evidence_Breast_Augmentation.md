### Post-mastectomy breast implant revision — November 2026

*Filename note: the historical filename (`…_Breast_Augmentation`) is kept so existing links keep working. The repository covers implant **revision and reconstruction after mastectomy**, not cosmetic augmentation.*

## Purpose and method

This file lists the evidence behind the modules. **Dose sources are kept separately** in [DOSING_SOURCES.md](DOSING_SOURCES.md). Every dose, rate, maximum, interval or threshold in the modules is tagged to that registry, and only guidelines and consensus statements, FDA labels and score-validation papers are accepted there.

The work is a **narrative synthesis**, not a systematic review. No protocol was registered, no systematic search was run, and no formal risk-of-bias assessment was done. The certainty labels in §9 are the author's GRADE-style judgments, not formal GRADE evidence profiles.

**Citation audit (v2.0, November 2026).** The v1.0 reference list was checked item by item against PubMed, publisher and repository records:
- **14 of 24** v1.0 entries could not be matched to any published article and were removed.
- **9** pointed to real papers or guidelines but had the wrong year, journal, title or edition. They are corrected below. One of these, "Adams WP et al., *PRS* 2020," matches no paper and was replaced with Adams's actual 2000, 2001 and 2006 papers.
- **1** (Apfel 1999) was correct apart from a missing year.
- Where a DOI or PMID could not be confirmed from a primary record, it is left out.

**November 2026 update.** Current guidelines were added: the fifth PONV consensus (23a), the 2026 ASA local/regional guideline (9a), the ASRA ketamine consensus (18a), the STOP-Bang and Aldrete validation papers (28a–c), the ASA monitoring standard (28d) and the CDC 2022 opioid guideline (28e). Kopp 2025 (ASRA antithrombotic, fifth edition) is the operative antithrombotic guideline. The removed references are listed in §10 for transparency.

19. Verret M, Lauzier F, Zarychanski R, et al. Perioperative use of gabapentinoids for the management of postoperative acute pain: a systematic review and meta-analysis. *Anesthesiology*. 2020;133(2):265-279. doi:10.1097/ALN.0000000000003428. PMID 32667154
20. Kharasch ED, Clark JD, Kheterpal S. Perioperative gabapentinoids: deflating the bubble. *Anesthesiology*. 2020;133(2):251-254. doi:10.1097/ALN.0000000000003394. PMID 32667153

**Summary.** Compared with opioid-inclusive anesthesia, opioid-free anesthesia gives similar early pain scores and less PONV (ref 11). In the largest multicenter RCT, dexmedetomidine-based OFA had **more** of the composite adverse outcome (78% vs 67%, driven by hypoxemia) and 5 cases of severe bradycardia, and the trial was stopped early; PONV and opioid use were lower (ref 10). *v2.0 removed* the v1.0 consensus statement that OFA works "without compromising hemodynamics." For IV lidocaine, the consensus is ≤1.5 mg/kg/h on ideal body weight, ≤24 h, and not concurrently with other local-anesthetic interventions (ref 17; [S1](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s1)). Routine gabapentinoids are not supported (refs 19–20).

**Notes on guideline currency.**
- **PONV:** the fifth consensus (ref 23a) recommends two prophylactic interventions for adults at risk. This repository's agent counts (two for 1–2 Apfel factors, three to four for ≥3; [S7](DOSING_SOURCES.md#s7)) follow the fourth consensus algorithm (ref 23), which secondary summaries indicate the fifth retains for higher-risk adults. The fifth's full dose table was not reviewed in November 2026.
- No 2020 or 2023 update of the ERAS breast reconstruction guideline was found. The 2017 paper (ref 21) is the operative document, although an ERAS Society check is still advisable.
- PROSPECT 2020 (ref 22) recommended preoperative gabapentinoids. That recommendation **conflicts** with the later evidence in refs 19–20, which this repository follows. An updated PROSPECT review for major oncological breast surgery was published online in *Anaesthesia* in August 2026 (Desai et al., doi:10.1111/anae.70313). Its recommendations have **not yet been reviewed** for this repository.
- The current ASA postanesthetic care guideline is the 2013 report (ref 27). The "2022" ASA guideline cited in v1.0 does not exist.

## 9. Summary of evidence certainty (author's judgment)

| Element | Certainty | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| PECS/serratus blocks reduce early pain and opioid use | **Moderate** | Multiple meta-analyses (refs 4–5); heterogeneity; benefit concentrated early |
| Opioid-free GA (vs opioid-inclusive) reduces PONV | **Moderate** | Meta-analyses (refs 11–12) |
| Dex-based OFA is as safe as opioid-based GA | **Not supported** | POFA found more hypoxemia and bradycardia (ref 10) |
| Multi-agent PONV prophylaxis by risk | **High** | Consensus guidelines + large factorial RCT (refs 23, 23a, 25) |
| Acetaminophen + NSAID foundation | **Moderate–High** | ERAS/PROSPECT (refs 21–22) |
| Routine gabapentinoids | **Not recommended** | Refs 19–20 |
| Antibiotic pocket irrigation / no-touch funnel | **Low** | In vitro + observational (refs 31, 36–37) |
| Capsulectomy extent; drain criteria | **Very low / expert opinion** | No sourced comparative data |
| Numeric outcome benchmarks for the three pathways | **None** | Removed in v2.0; no source |

## 11. Evidence statements not retained

The following are not retained, because a general guideline label cannot establish them and they need source-specific assessment:
- universal superiority of PECS blocks;
- universal safety or superiority of opioid-free anesthesia;
- fixed percentage improvements;
- informal A/B certainty grades;
- claims of consensus for specific irrigation or capsulectomy strategies.

For a separate narrative review with a broader bibliography, see the [Breast Reconstruction Analgesia Review](https://github.com/collingeorge/Breast-Reconstruction-Analgesia-Review). That project is not an institutional protocol either.
