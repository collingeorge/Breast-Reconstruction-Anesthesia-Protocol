# Regional analgesia with multimodal care: discussion module

> Independent educational work. Not a clinical protocol, prescription, patient handout, or validated decision aid. This revision is a draft pending clinical and publication review.

Pectoral and related fascial plane blocks are procedure-dependent analgesic techniques. Published evidence from one breast surgery population should not be treated as proof of superiority in all implant revision procedures. Incomplete coverage and block-related harms remain possible.

This module does not rank blocks above all alternative strategies and does not provide injection technique, local anesthetic dose, adjuvant dose, or a rescue algorithm.

**Local anesthetic overlap:** the earlier suggestion that IV lidocaine could routinely accompany a block with conservative dosing has been removed. The international consensus advises against overlapping IV lidocaine with other local anesthetic interventions and specifies separation intervals. Cumulative toxicity involves different local anesthetics as well as different routes. A milligram total alone does not establish safety.

## Sources and limits

- [Foo et al., 2021: IV lidocaine consensus](https://associationofanaesthetists-publications.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/anae.15270).
- [Beloeil et al., 2021: POFA randomized trial](https://pubs.asahq.org/anesthesiology/article-pdf/134/4/541/512443/20210400.0-00011.pdf).
- [Verret et al., 2020: perioperative gabapentinoid meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32667154/).

These sources do not validate an implant-revision-specific regimen. Source-specific populations, comparators, follow-up, and adverse effects must be checked before generalization.
